## How this tabletop was planned
I created and executed this tabletop following CISA **Cybersecurity Tabletop Exercise Tips** [1] using a scenario adapted from CIS **Six Scenarios to Help Prepare Your Cybersecurity Team** [2] second exercise.   
The exercise was played by five players (plus the exercise leader/facilitator), each one representing a role from the following list: SOC Analyst, IT & infrastructure administrator, CISO, HR lead, financial department lead.
Each player received a description of his responsabilities and strongest interests. 

## Scenario
> An employee within your organization from the financial department used the company’s digital camera for business purposes.  In the course of doing so, they took a scenic photograph that they then loaded onto their  personal computer by inserting the SD card. The SD card was infected with malware while connected to the employee’s personal computer. When re-inserted into a company machine, it infected the organization’s system with the same malware.  The EDR installed at the infected machine triggered a alert.
> 
> What is your response?  

## Discussion questions
- Who within the organization would you need to notify?
- How would your organization identify and respond to malware infecting your system through this vector?
	- What is the process for identifying the infection vector?
- What other devices could present similar threats?
- What should management do?
	- Must the infected machine be isolated immediately?
	- How to communicate the incident to other departments without generating panic?
- How can you prevent this from occurring again?
	- Does your organization have training and policies in place to prevent this?
	- Do policies apply to all storage devices?  

A [timeline of events with more questions]() was created using a generative AI to enhance the exercise realism and details.
 

## Lessons learned and IRP-PB updates
- Each department leader have a strong interest bias in decision-making.
- The playbook must have steps related to stakeholders warning the IT sector about suspicious behavior.
- The HR personell plays an important role in communication decisions to prevent organization scandals and protect incident involved stakeholders.
- Under pressure, emotions can interfere in decision taking. That way, teamwork has enormous importance.
